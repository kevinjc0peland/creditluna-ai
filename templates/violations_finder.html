What your answers raise

These are issues to check against your records, in three strengths. A flag is not a conclusion — a court or regulator decides that, from documents.

Next steps, in order.
  • Keep everything: dated letters and envelopes, call logs with times, and screenshots of the tradeline from each bureau.
  • Draft your dispute or validation letter in the Letter workspace, then review and send it yourself. Keep a copy of what you send.
  • Complain to the CFPB — it routes to the company and builds a record. The BBB and your state attorney general take the same kinds of complaints.
  • Several Tier 1 flags together are worth a consult with a consumer-law attorney (NACA's finder is a common starting point).
  • Read the CreditLuna guides for the background behind each rule.

Education only — CreditLuna never files, disputes or negotiates for anyone. Compiled from the federal statutes as written; confirm any date, amount or citation against your own documents before it goes in a letter.
